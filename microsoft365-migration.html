NEW WAVE GROUP / 2023 TO 2025

The Microsoft 365 migration

This was one of my most challenging projects and one of the projects I am most proud of. It involved much more than moving files from the servers.

01 / MY ROLE

Working with the PM and the migration team

I led the 12-person AD-to-Microsoft 365, Entra ID and OneDrive migration team. I worked with the PM on the pre-study: what data we had, which tools to use, how to do the work and where to start.

The wider group had more than 5,000 users. Central IT was in Sweden, Belgium and the Netherlands, with local IT contacts at the individual companies and sites.

I also led separate teams for the KACE-to-Intune migration and Exchange mail. They were different projects with different teams.

02 / PLANNING

People had different data and different deadlines.

The project became bigger than expected. Looking at the servers did not show how much data departments such as finance actually had, or how people were using local storage and existing cloud storage.

Finance had reporting dates. The creative teams had catalogue deadlines. I brought those dates into the planning with the PM so we could decide who to move and when.

During the file transfer, people needed to understand when they could not work on or change the source files. That took patience. They still had work to do, and everyone reacted differently.

03 / CHANGING THE PLAN

We had to check the servers as well as the schedule.

Server support dates and disk health affected the migration order. In one case, a company was scheduled months ahead, but a storage failure meant we needed to deal with it immediately.

I worked with the PM to adjust the plan, prioritize the affected company and decide how to carry out the work.

04 / HANDOVER

I made the plan so other people could do it.

I could not carry out every migration myself. I documented the process so the whole team could use it and continue the work. That included what needed to happen to the on-premise servers when they were shut down.

One of the biggest things I learned was to bring technical people into the project earlier. The PM and management do not know everything from the start. We needed to fill those gaps together before execution.
