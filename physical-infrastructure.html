RACKS, CABLING AND DOCUMENTATION

I need to know where the cable goes.

When something needs fixing, time matters. I want the cables organized, tested and labelled so I can find the connection and someone else can do the same.

01 / CABLING

Test the cables and keep the documentation.

I test cables and keep the results. Even when someone tells me a cable has been tested, I want the record. I also want clear labels on the equipment and a cable name that connects to the documentation.

My cable comb

I redesigned a cable comb and had a friend print it for me. It twists the outer layer of cables while keeping them accessible, so I can find and remove a faulty cable.

CONTROL4 AND LIGHTING

Planning and programming the installation

My Control4 work included lighting, AV routing and multi-room audio. I planned network and lighting layouts and documented the dimmers, switches and other equipment used in the installation.

The lighting plan sets out the keypads, dry-contact module, eight-channel adaptive dimmers and 48V bus power supply. I grouped the indoor and outdoor lighting zones and marked the cable routes before installation.

The network and AV plan shows the access points, displays, cameras, speaker connections and double Cat6 outlets. I worked from the plans through installation, programming, testing and customer handover.

INSTALLATION PLANNING

I plan it on paper first.

I plan the cable routes, access point positions, speakers and equipment before starting the installation. These are my planning prints and notes.

02 / RECORDS

I use Excel on site, then NetBox.

While I am working, I record the cable name, switch and ports in Excel. Afterwards I enter or import the information into NetBox, check how the connections fit together and go back to double-check anything that looks wrong.

I used this method at New Wave. It lets me capture the information while doing the physical work, then sit down and work through the records afterwards.

03 / SERVER ROOMS

Equipment, cooling and backup work

At New Wave I worked in small server-room environments, deploying and changing equipment, maintaining racks and cables, working with UPS equipment and handling backup operations. I was one of the two people responsible for backups, including Arcserve and Dell tape equipment.

After an air-conditioning failure, we revised the emergency cooling procedures and added room monitoring.

04 / VENDORS

What can we do together to make it work?

When a delivery or a promised fix is missing, I want to find the problem and a solution. Can we get the parts elsewhere? Is there a temporary fix? When will the permanent fix be ready?

I ask for the commitment in writing, with a date. If it is not delivered, I escalate it. I can be firm without losing my temper or blaming people.

05 / HANDOVER

Someone may need to take over tomorrow.

I keep the documentation and labels up to date and teach people what I know. The work should not depend on me being there.

I cannot publish all of my work because of NDAs, client confidentiality and other restrictions.
