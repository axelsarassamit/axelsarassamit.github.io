SWEET COMMUNICATION / BANGKOK / SEPTEMBER 2025 TO FEBRUARY 2026

I built the infrastructure and supported it.

I took two old computers and built a Proxmox environment using open-source software. The budget was limited, so I worked with the equipment available and automated as much of the routine work as I could.

ServersIdentity and computersAutomation and monitoringDocumentation

01 / SERVERS

Two Proxmox servers

All hosted services ran in VMs across the two Proxmox servers. I built and administered the environment for 61 client computers, alongside daily support.

The services I used

FreeIPA / SSSDCentral identity
Ansible / SemaphoreWorkstation setup and administration
ZabbixMonitoring and alerts
WireGuardRemote access
NetBox / BookStackInventory and documentation
JenkinsCI/CD and automation jobs

02 / IDENTITY AND COMPUTERS

Central administration

I used FreeIPA with SSSD for the Linux computers, including a directory replica. In December 2025, the environment had 57 Xubuntu 24.04 LTS computers and four Windows endpoints.

I worked on centralized home directories using NFS and FreeIPA. That rollout was still ongoing. I also handled network segmentation, WireGuard remote access and Google Workspace administration.

03 / AUTOMATION AND MONITORING

I could not spend all day running around fixing computers.

I used Ansible, Semaphore and scripts for repeatable setup and maintenance. This work also led to Atomator, my Linux administration project and Android companion.

Zabbix monitored the infrastructure and computers. NetBox held the network and device records, and BookStack held the procedures and knowledge base.

Trying AI in the infrastructure

I wanted to see whether I could spot connectivity problems earlier and prepare the computers for them. I experimented with AI-assisted troubleshooting and changing client DNS settings when I could not replace the upstream equipment.

This was a learning project. A self-healing network is still something I want to build.

04 / DOCUMENTATION AND SUPPORT

Someone else needs to be able to take over.

I wrote the infrastructure map, service inventory, installation instructions, onboarding and offboarding procedures, troubleshooting guides and IT-policy material. The user guides were in English and Swedish.

The guides helped users check a problem and explain whether it affected one computer or the wider office. My day-to-day work also included access administration, patching, maintenance and coordination with vendors and the internet provider.
