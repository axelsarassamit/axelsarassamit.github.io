Privacy policy

Your photos. Your account. Your choices.

Effective 27 October 2026. Publisher: Axel Loosme. This policy covers the Pictalune Windows app and these app information pages.

Data read and sent to Google

When you choose local folders, Pictalune scans their filenames, paths, sizes, timestamps and file contents to calculate hashes and prepare an upload preview. Only when you start uploading does the app send the selected original media, filenames and requested album names to your Google Photos account. Google processes that data under Google's Privacy Policy. Uploading uses your Google Photos storage.

Google sign-in opens in your browser. Pictalune receives authorization tokens, your Google account identifier and email address. These identify the account and associate its local upload receipts correctly. Pictalune does not receive or store your Google password.

Permissions and Limited Use

The app requests permission to add media and albums to Google Photos and read media created by this app. It also requests OpenID account identity and email access. Reading app-created media is used for user-requested cloud verification before cleanup. It does not provide access to browse your entire Google Photos library.

Pictalune follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide the upload, album, account and verification features you request. It is not sold, used for advertising, supplied to data brokers or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Local storage and retention

Tokens and the app's Google connection configuration are protected locally with Windows DPAPI. Local SQLite history contains account identifiers, file paths, SHA-256 hashes, sizes, timestamps, Google media IDs and receipt links, album memberships and results. Verification records also store the time, media ID and comparison outcome. This information remains on your PC until you remove it. The original PhotoBridge data folder name is retained for compatibility. Local receipts are not uploaded to the developer or GitHub.

You can explicitly export history to a CSV file and are responsible for where you save or share that export.

Cloud verification and local cleanup

When you request verification, Pictalune retrieves the current Google media item and streams a Google download into a SHA-256 and size comparison. It does not save an extra local copy of those downloaded bytes. Only files matching a fresh cloud download exactly can be selected for cleanup. The check is repeated before removal.

Cleanup requires your separate file selection and confirmation. Eligible local files move to the Windows Recycle Bin. Pictalune does not permanently delete originals or empty the Recycle Bin. Changed, unavailable or mismatched cloud copies leave the local original untouched. Google may alter metadata or transcode media, preventing an exact match. Recovery and retention in the Recycle Bin are controlled by Windows.

Data sharing and developer access

Chosen media is transferred directly to Google, not through a developer-operated server. Pictalune has no developer analytics, advertising or automatic developer telemetry. The developer cannot inspect your Google account, pictures, tokens or local history through the app. Opening receipt links launches Google Photos in your browser. Google and Microsoft process account, download and Store interactions under their own policies.

Disconnecting and deleting data

Disconnecting in the app removes its local token and account record. You can revoke its Google grant through Google Account permissions. Disconnecting does not delete your Google Photos media or the local upload history.

To erase local history and verification records, close Pictalune and remove its PhotoBridge folder from your Windows Local AppData directory. Export history first if you want a copy. Removing receipts also removes the app's ability to recognize those prior uploads. You manage and delete cloud media through Google Photos. Remove any separately exported CSV files yourself if you no longer need them.

This website

These static app pages do not add tracking, cookies, forms, third-party fonts or analytics. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process ordinary connection information under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following external links connects you to those providers.

Questions and changes

For privacy or support questions, contact Axel Loosme through the contact page. Future policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date. Pictalune is currently a private test release; Google OAuth verification and public release are not complete.
