Terms of use

Use with care. Keep control.

Effective 27 October 2026. Pictalune is independently developed by Axel Loosme. These terms describe its current private testing stage.

Private testing

Pictalune is provided to invited testers for personal evaluation. Microsoft Store availability and Google sign-in may be restricted to approved accounts. The app is not yet a general public release or a Google-verified integration. These pages do not grant access to the private test.

Your media and accounts

Use Pictalune only with media and accounts you have permission to access and upload. You retain your rights to your media. You are responsible for complying with applicable rules and the terms of Google Photos and Microsoft Store. Pictalune does not sell Google storage or guarantee available capacity.

Uploads and duplicates

Review selected folders before starting. Uploads may fail because of network problems, account restrictions, storage limits or unsupported formats. Reuse depends on local receipts from the same app connection and account. It does not detect all pre-existing photos in your Google Photos library.

Cleanup and backups

Test with disposable copies first and keep an independent backup of important originals. Cloud verification is a point-in-time comparison, not a promise of permanent cloud retention. Google can change or remove media after a check. Only exact matches qualify for separately confirmed Recycle Bin cleanup. Windows controls recovery and retention; do not empty the Recycle Bin until you have checked what you need.

Privacy and third parties

The Pictalune Privacy Policy explains the app's handling of media, account information and local history. Google Photos and Microsoft Store are separate services with their own terms and policies. Pictalune is not affiliated with Google or Microsoft.

Support and changes

Report problems through Axel Loosme's contact page. Features and test availability may change as issues are found. Stop using the app if it does not behave as expected, and preserve your original files and history while seeking help.
