NEW WAVE GROUP / ORREFORS KOSTA BODA / JANUARY 2023 TO FEBRUARY 2025

Infrastructure and site IT at New Wave

My work covered the central IT environment and the sites around Kosta: manufacturing, hotels, shops, offices, warehouses and the new arena. I worked with the equipment on site and with colleagues and local IT contacts across the group.

01 / SITE IT

Different sites had different needs.

Kosta Boda Art Hotel reopening

I supported the IT for the hotel reopening. The work covered hotel systems, POS and payment terminals, guest and staff networks, room connectivity, access and key-card systems, conference AV and printing.

The hospitality environment also included Hotsoft PMS, restaurant and kitchen-display systems and spa integrations.

The new CRAFT Arena

The arena was a new venue build. My work included the network, Wi-Fi and POS used for visitor-facing operations.

Manufacturing, shops and warehouses

I supported the infrastructure around glass manufacturing, the factory shop, outlet, offices and warehouse operations. That included connectivity and barcode and label devices. I worked with the local IT contacts and vendors on the business applications.

02 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Servers, cables, power and backups

I worked with Dell servers, storage, VMware vSphere/ESXi, Windows Server and Active Directory, alongside network equipment, racks, cables and UPS equipment.

I used Fluke and SignalTEK tools in my network work, tested and replaced cables, and kept the connection records up to date. On site I recorded the cable names, switches and ports in Excel, then worked through the records in NetBox.

Backup work

I led the two-person backup team. The work included Arcserve backup administration, Dell tape equipment, restore drills and checking recovery objectives.

Emergency response

I was part of the emergency team for critical IT incidents. After an air-conditioning failure, we revised the emergency cooling procedures and added room monitoring.

03 / TEAMS AND COORDINATION

Working across the companies

Central IT was based in Sweden, Belgium and the Netherlands. Individual companies and sites had their own IT contacts. I needed patience and clear communication to get work done with people outside my direct reporting line.

I worked with PMO and team coordinators on the separate projects. My responsibilities included MacOps, the Microsoft 365 migration, KACE-to-Intune migration, backups, printing and Exchange mail, with different teams for different work.

I also used my purchasing and supplier experience to find better prices and purchasing channels for IT parts, cables and materials.
